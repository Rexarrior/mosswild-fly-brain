This is a chronological lab notebook: pending actions below describe their status
at the time of writing. The final results and current reproduction instructions
are in [Mosswild.md](Mosswild.md). Ten complete improvement cycles are recorded
in `BrainBiome-iterations.json`; an interrupted partial run is excluded.

All iterations use the actual Unreal actors and full Metal connectomes. Baseline is a 600-second real-time PIE capture on the old map. New biome audit mode advances ecology by a fixed 0.4 seconds only after a completed neural batch; 600 simulated seconds per cycle. The initial neural window was 40 ms; from cycle 2 it is 20 ms, as recorded below. This avoids frame-rate-dependent comparisons, and is not biological real-time.

Planned staged changes (subject to measurements): resource utility/hysteresis; water priorities; nest economy; bounded resource memory; recruitment trails; separation/obstacle escape; alignment/cohesion; combat risk/alarm; calibrated learned motor readout; metric-guided joint tuning. Each cycle must retain its configuration, 600-second trace and analysis. Failed or rejected candidates remain in the record. Final changes must be applied to the interactive project and checked in PIE.
